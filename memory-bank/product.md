# Product Context

This React application serves as a personalized marketing landing page for a leather goods company. The product solves the challenge of creating targeted, time-sensitive marketing campaigns by implementing dynamic personalization based on URL parameters.

The application addresses several key business needs:
- Creating urgency through time-limited offers
- Personalizing content for different target audiences
- Showcasing premium leather products through video content
- Capturing leads through strategic call-to-action placement

The user experience is designed to feel exclusive and invitation-only, with personalized branding, urgency timers, and compelling messaging about market leadership and legacy building.

Key user flows include:
1. Access via personalized URL with encoded data
2. Immediate personalization of content based on brand and reader information
3. Engagement with video content and strategic calls-to-action
4. Response to urgency elements and time-sensitive offers
